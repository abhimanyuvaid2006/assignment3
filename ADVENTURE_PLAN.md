# Adventure Game Plan

## Story Paths

### Path 1: Explore the Beach
- Decision Point 1: You find a broken boat and a signal fire pit. What do you do?
  - Option A: Fix the boat  (leads to Decision Point 2)
  - Option B: Light the signal (rescued by a passing ship)

- Decision Point 2: The boat is fixed but a storm is coming. What do you do?
  - Option A: Wait for the storm to pass (storm passes, you sail home safely)
  - Option B: Sail through the storm (boat capsizes, you swim back to shore)



### Path 2: Go Into the Jungle
- Decision Point 1: You hear water and spot some fruit trees. What do you do?
  - Option A: Follow the sound of water (leads to Decision Point 2)
  - Option B: Climb a tree for fruit (you eat, gain energy, find a trail home)

- Decision Point 2: You find a freshwater river. Which way do you follow it?
  - Option A: Follow it upstream (you find a hidden village and are helped home)
  - Option B: Follow it downstream (it leads to the ocean, you signal a boat)



### Path 3: Climb the Mountain
- Decision Point 1: Halfway up you find a cave and an old radio. What do you do?
  - Option A: Rest in the cave (leads to Decision Point 2)
  - Option B: Try the radio (you reach a coast guard, get rescued)

- Decision Point 2: Inside the cave you find supplies left by someone else. What do you do?
  - Option A: Take the supplies and keep climbing(you reach the top, spot a nearby island with people)
  - Option B: Stay in the cave and wait (a rescue helicopter spots the cave entrance)
